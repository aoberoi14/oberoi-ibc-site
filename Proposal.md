# PROPOSAL.md — Oberoi IBC Website

## Client Brief

**Client:** Oberoi IBC India Pvt. Ltd — a full-service advertising agency based in Mumbai,
Maharashtra, founded in 1995. The agency creates TVCs, brand campaigns, and creative content
for Indian businesses, working with celebrities and international productions.

**Purpose:** To give Oberoi IBC a clean, professional web presence that communicates their
legacy, showcases their work, and converts visiting brands into leads.

**Audience:**
- Brand clients looking for an agency to handle their advertising and campaigns
- Other agencies exploring collaboration or partnerships
- Individuals and businesses looking to produce ads or TVCs

**Key action:** Fill out a contact/interest form expressing their brand's needs and what
they are looking for from the agency.

**Pages/sections needed:**
- Homepage — agency intro, reel or hero visual, quick value proposition
- About — agency history (founded 1995), team, ethos, and what sets them apart
- Work — showcase of past campaigns and TVCs (linked to YouTube)
- Contact — interest form where potential clients describe their brand and requirements

**Content status:**
- YouTube channel available with campaign work: https://www.youtube.com/@Oberoiibc
- Instagram page available: https://www.instagram.com/oberoiibc
- Logo, brand colors, and additional campaign images to be confirmed with client
- Contact details available: brands@oberoiibc.com / +91 9819337888

**Style preferences:** Minimal and clean but bold. Think editorial and cinematic — the kind
of aesthetic that signals a serious creative agency without being flashy or overcrowded.

**Inspiration sites:**
- To be added after reviewing 3 to 5 reference agency sites with client
